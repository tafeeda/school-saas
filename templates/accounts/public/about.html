About EduPortal

EduPortal by Mutafs Global Technologies is a modern, cloud-based school management system designed to simplify academic operations, improve transparency, and enhance communication between schools, teachers, students, and parents.

From result management and student records to lesson notes and analytics, EduPortal provides a complete digital solution for schools of all sizes.

Smart Results

Automated grading, ranking, and result publication with secure student access.

Multi-School SaaS

Manage multiple schools from a single platform with full control.

Secure Access

Role-based access for admins, teachers, students, and parents.
